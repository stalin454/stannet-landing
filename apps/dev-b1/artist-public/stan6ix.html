PROFILE / ENCYCLOPEDIC BIOGRAPHY

Biography

STAN6IX is a fictional six-member virtual hip-hop collective developed as part of StanNet Music. Its concept blends Bronx-inspired hip-hop imagery with multicultural characters and sounds drawing on boom bap, electronic rap, R&B, synthwave and South Asian rhythms.

The project revolves around six distinct stage characters: Zay Rivers, Kai Chen, Juno Park, Ace Morgan, Noah Berg and Arjun Singh. Each character has a fictional background, musical specialty and visual identity. Together they explore storytelling about family, belonging, friendship, resilience and the ambition to create something lasting.

STAN6IX is a virtual creative project, not a group of six real-life performers. Biographical details attached to its characters are fictional. This official profile records the project's characters, authorized artwork and confirmed releases as they become available.
